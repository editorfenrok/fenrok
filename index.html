About Me

Who is Fenrok?

I got into editing because I genuinely love making content look and feel incredible. Three years later I've worked with creators across gaming, documentary, anime, podcast, reaction and educational content — channels anywhere from brand new to over 6 million subscribers.

Every editor can cut a video. What I care about is whether it actually holds attention, whether it fits the creator's voice, and whether it gets delivered on time. That's what keeps clients coming back.

I work across YouTube long-form, Reels, Shorts, ads and promotional content. Whatever your niche, I've probably edited something in it.
